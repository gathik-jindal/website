Featured research · 2026

Published

SeeIntent

Real-time multimodal intent recognition in mixed reality using a spatio-temporal LLM.

An end-to-end pipeline that predicts what a headset wearer is about to do. It fuses YOLOv11n object detection on the passthrough feed, a CPU-light XGBoost facial-emotion classifier (78.8% test accuracy on FACS blendshapes), and gaze-based fixation estimation into a rolling history buffer that an LLM reasons over. Evaluated on EPIC-KITCHENS and an in-the-wild Meta Quest pilot dataset.

Presented at ICPR 2026, ETTAC workshop. With Jayant Sharma, Beryl Gnanaraj, and Jaya Sreevalsan-Nair, Graphics-Visualization-Computing Lab, IIIT Bangalore.

ML competition · 2026

Amazon ML Challenge '26

Business Entity Resolution

Find every record that describes the same business, across 1.73M entities and 9.97M noisy look-alike records from the US, India, and France. A gradient-boosted cascade scores candidate pairs, fine-tuned cross-encoders re-read the uncertain 23.5%, and an expected-F0.5 rule picks each entity's matches. Finished in the top 50 of 32,000 teams (0.9906 on the public leaderboard), earning a pre-placement interview for an ML Scientist role at Amazon.

Digital logic · 2023

Moore Machine Simulator

A simulator for Moore machines, finite state machines whose outputs depend only on the current state. A Python engine built on SimPy runs discrete-event simulations of clocks, counters, latches, and combinational blocks, wired together with overloaded operators, and plots the waveforms. A Java drag-and-drop GUI lets you draw the circuit and generates the Python for it.
